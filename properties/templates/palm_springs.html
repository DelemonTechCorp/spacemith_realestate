Palm Springs by Nakheel – Project Overview

Palm Springs by Nakheel is a master-planned residential community in Jebel Ali, Dubai, offering contemporary villas and townhouses in a thoughtfully designed environment. Developed by Nakheel, one of Dubai's leading master developers, this residential destination combines modern architecture, natural surroundings, and family-focused amenities.

Located near Palm Jebel Ali, the development offers convenient access to Sheikh Zayed Road and major Dubai destinations. Featuring 3- and 4-bedroom townhouses alongside 4- and 5-bedroom villas, Palm Springs provides attractive options for families, homeowners, and property investors seeking premium residences in Dubai.

Palm Springs is a large-scale residential development spanning approximately 3.8 million square metres, with 15 planned neighbourhoods and a projected population of almost 50,000 residents.

The masterplan incorporates landscaped parks, pedestrian-friendly pathways, recreational facilities, and essential community infrastructure. Canyon Park, a central landscaped green corridor, connects residential neighbourhoods with outdoor leisure destinations.

Palm Springs by Nakheel Payment Plan

Flexible 60/40 Payment Plan

Palm Springs by Nakheel offers a 60/40 payment plan, with a 10% down payment upon booking, 50% payable in scheduled instalments during construction, and the remaining 40% due upon handover. The project is expected to be completed in Q4 2030. Payment terms are subject to final developer confirmation.
