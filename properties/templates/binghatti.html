Lifestyle

Lifestyle At Binghatti Starfall

Life at Binghatti Starfall is set around the Creek promenade and Culture Village Park – a waterfront setting for morning walks and weekend strolls, just steps from home, with everyday retail, pharmacies and grocery stores within walking distance of the tower.

Every residence, from studio to 2 bedroom, shares the same glazed facades, fitted kitchens and considered storage that define Binghatti's design language, with living and dining spaces lined up to the windows to maximise natural light and views toward the Creek or the Downtown skyline.

Wellness spaces sit at podium level for residents to unwind away from the street, while ground-level play areas and shaded seating support family living – all within minutes of Business Bay and the Burj Khalifa district.
