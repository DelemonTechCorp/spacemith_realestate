About The Project

About Kore by Imtiaz at Dubai Land Residence Complex

Kore is a wellness-focused development where modern comfort meets active, balanced living, with contemporary apartments and well-planned shared spaces.

Developed by Imtiaz Developments in Dubai Land Residence Complex (DLRC), Dubailand, Kore offers studio, one- and two-bedroom homes for residents who value an active lifestyle, as well as buyers exploring Dubai's off-plan market.

The name KORE stands for the centre of strength, stability and movement, and puts personal well-being at the heart of everyday living.
