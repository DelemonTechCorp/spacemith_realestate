A New Vision of Contemporary Family Living

Welcome to Greenwoods by Aldar, an upcoming residential development in Dubai Silicon Oasis, thoughtfully envisioned to combine modern living with peaceful green surroundings.

Developed by Aldar Properties, the community introduces a selection of elegant villas, twin villas and townhouses designed to complement modern family lifestyles.

With a focus on landscaped open spaces, welcoming neighbourhoods and convenient connectivity, Greenwoods offers a refreshing residential environment where everyday comfort meets natural beauty.

Location & Connectivity

Greenwoods by Aldar Location in Dubai Silicon Oasis

Greenwoods is planned beside the established Dubai Silicon Oasis district, on Dubai's eastern growth corridor opposite Nad Al Sheba. Schools, healthcare and shopping are minutes away, with fast road links into the rest of the city.

  • E311
    Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road Direct route north to Sharjah and south towards Jebel Ali
  • E66
    Dubai–Al Ain Road Quick access to Downtown Dubai and Business Bay
  • Dubai Metro Blue Line (planned) Planned to serve Dubai Silicon Oasis, linking the community to the wider Metro network
