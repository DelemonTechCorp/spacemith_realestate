Lifestyle

Lifestyle At Ellington Master Community Dubai

Life at Al Yalayis 1 is built around the community's crystal water body and the landscaped parks that surround it – spaces designed for morning walks, weekend gatherings and children playing outdoors within a short stroll of home.

Every residence, whether an apartment, townhouse or standalone villa, shares the same fully fitted kitchens, premium hardware and contemporary interiors that define Ellington Properties' design language, while the low-density masterplan keeps streets quiet and communal areas uncrowded.

Family-oriented amenities and wellness facilities are woven throughout the community, so residents can move between home, park and shared spaces without leaving the neighbourhood – the resort-inspired experience Ellington is positioning Al Yalayis 1 around.
