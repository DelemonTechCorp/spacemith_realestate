Lifestyle

Waterfront Lifestyle At Shahrukhz Residences

The range of amenities at Shahrukhz Residences extends beyond conventional residential facilities. Danube states that the project will provide more than 40 amenities designed around family life, recreation, fitness, relaxation and entertainment.

Infinity 33 introduces a beachside leisure experience, while King's Club Deck provides a private rooftop environment overlooking the sea. Residents will also have access to an indoor gym with waterfront views.

One of the more futuristic concepts is the Longevity Lounge, planned to include cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, red-light therapy, compression therapy and smart wellness assessment facilities – a wellness-led approach that reflects how next-generation residential developments are combining property, lifestyle and technology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Shahrukhz Residences by Danube located?

Shahrukhz Residences by Danube is located in Dubai Maritime City, a growing waterfront district positioned close to Port Rashid, Jumeirah Beach, Dubai World Trade Centre, Downtown Dubai and Dubai International Airport.

What types of properties are available at Shahrukhz Residences?

The project offers a broad residential mix including studios, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, 3-bedroom and 4-bedroom residences, along with premium penthouses. Buyers can choose according to space requirements, views and investment objectives.

What is the Shahrukhz Residences payment plan?

The current payment plan is presented as a 30/70 structure, with 30% payable before handover and 70% payable at handover. Buyers should confirm the latest terms directly before reserving a unit because payment structures can be updated.

When is the Shahrukhz Residences handover?

The current expected completion date is Q4 2029. Buyers should verify the latest construction and delivery information at the time of purchase.

Is Shahrukhz Residences a good investment?

Shahrukhz Residences may appeal to buyers seeking exposure to Dubai Maritime City, waterfront real estate and a new off-plan development by Danube Properties. Potential investors should evaluate unit price, floor plan, view, rental demand, service charges, future supply and resale conditions rather than relying only on projected returns.
